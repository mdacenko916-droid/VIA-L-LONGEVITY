ADAM-L is a wellness app for men from 40 going through andropause. The user records how they slept, how much energy they had and how they felt; the app describes, in plain language, what appears connected to what and suggests one lifestyle change to try. A wearable is optional: the app works fully on manually entered data; Apple Health only fills those fields in.

It does not diagnose, treat or prevent disease and is not a medical device. The disclaimer is shown before first use and repeated on every result.

Relation to our other app VIA-L (same developer): ADAM-L is a separate product for a different audience (men, andropause). Content, workouts, library shelf, design and onboarding are male-specific; there are no cycle, perimenopause or menopause features. Same privacy model and the same backend.

Access: right after the first-launch disclaimer the paywall offers ADAM-L Monthly and ADAM-L Yearly (auto-renewable, one group, each with a 3-day free trial with full access) and ADAM-L Lifetime (non-consumable). The paywall states above the plans that the first 3 days are free with full access and that payment is charged after 3 days unless cancelled in Apple ID settings. Restore Purchases is on the paywall. There is no login.

Library (bottom bar → Library): plain-language summaries of published studies; titles are visible to everyone, full texts are part of the ADAM-L subscription — no separate purchase, no code, no external payment links.

Daily workout card: text exercises with an optional voice guide using the device's built-in text-to-speech (no microphone).

"Together" (optional pairing): on the Today screen a user can invite one person with a 6-character one-time code. Each person sees only the other's display name, current weekly step (one of nine fixed lifestyle steps) and seven "done / not done" marks. No health data, analyses or free text are exchanged and there is no messaging. Either person can leave the pair at any time.

Lab results from a photo (Profile → My data → Lab tests → Scan from photo): the photo is sent only to read the numbers and is not stored; values are confirmed by the user and kept on the device.

Specialist (consumable IAPs adam_l_specialist_review — a written review — and adam_l_specialist_plan — 3-month recommendations): hidden when the device time zone is a US one — to see them, set e.g. Madrid, reopen the app, open the Specialist tab. Connecting to a personal specialist is optional and free: code FOUNDER, any email, tick consent, Connect. The code unlocks no paid feature; ongoing consultations are arranged and paid outside the app (Guideline 3.1.3(d)); the app shows no prices or payment links for them.

Health data: HealthKit is read-only and requested only after explaining its use. Data stays on the device; it is shared only with a specialist the user chose or bought a review from, with explicit consent, and is never used for advertising or sold.

7-bis. Health Data: Storage, Export and Deletion

Health data is handled separately from everything else, and the rules differ by product.

  • VIA-L (mobile app): your health data stays on your device. We keep no copy of it on our servers; text sent for an AI overview is transient. The generated overview alone is held for up to 72 hours under a random device identifier, so it can be retrieved if the response never reached the app, and is then deleted automatically. Separately, «Help improve VIA·L» is an opt-in, off by default: while on, the numbers of each finished day are sent under the same random device identifier so the app can be calibrated; free text is refused by the server, and «Delete my contribution» erases what was already sent.
  • VIA-L EXPERT (guidance with a specialist): your data is additionally stored in your record so your specialist can work with it.
  • Retention: health data in that record is deleted automatically 90 days after the guidance period ends. The record of service (dates of access, number of overviews and messages) is kept for 3 years as evidence for both you and your specialist.
  • Export: “Save backup” in the app writes an encrypted file that only your password opens.
  • Deletion: “Delete my health data” erases it immediately — on the device in VIA-L, and both on the device and in the specialist's record in VIA-L EXPERT. Only the record of service above remains. Washington residents: this is the deletion right under the My Health My Data Act, and it covers consumer health data, not merely the account.

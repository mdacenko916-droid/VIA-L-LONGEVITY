2. Scope

This policy covers all data processing performed by VIA-L across the following products and services:

  • Marketing website at via-l.com — informational pages and program presentations.
  • Coaching programs — mature vitality, active longevity, internal balance and lifestyle-optimization programs delivered remotely by a wellness guide.
  • VIA-L Interpreter — a software tool (web application) that analyses wearable-device biometry and self-reported markers and returns nutritional-support suggestions, using an AI model (Anthropic Claude). The Interpreter is offered in four tiers: VIO (free), PRO (subscription), PRO+EXPERT (subscription with written wellness-guide follow-up), ELITE (programme with weekly wellness-guide follow-up).

The Interpreter is not a medical device and does not perform medical diagnosis. The wellness guide is not a physician and does not provide medical treatment. See our Terms of Service for the full disclaimer.

  • Onboarding questionnaire (PRO+EXPERT / ELITE only): goals, lifestyle habits, historical personal wellness traits, dietary preferences, digestive comfort indicators and supplement routines. This information is sent to the wellness guide to inform written PDF summaries.

5. AI Processing Disclosure

The Interpreter generates its personalised analysis using a large-language-model service: Claude Haiku by Anthropic PBC (United States). When you reach the result step:

  • A summary of your inputs (biometry plus self-reported markers — never identity or contact data) is sent over HTTPS to a Cloudflare Worker hosted in the EU edge network, which proxies the request to Anthropic.
  • Per Anthropic's commercial terms, your data is not used to train Anthropic's models and is not retained beyond the time required to generate the response.
  • The AI output is advisory and educational. It does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis or prescription. You are encouraged to discuss any meaningful health decision with a qualified physician.
  • You can use the Interpreter in VIO mode (free, no AI) if you prefer not to have your data processed by an AI service. Manual scoring and local recommendations remain available.

This disclosure is provided in line with the transparency obligations of the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689), in particular Article 50 concerning AI systems that interact with natural persons.

6. Special Category and Biometric Data

The Interpreter and coaching programs necessarily involve special category data within the meaning of GDPR Article 9 (intimate wellness indicators and cycle metrics you choose to share, biometric data, and cyclical and physical well-being patterns via optional cycle-rhythm questions). Processing of this data is:

  • Based on your explicit consent, given at the start of each session and stored alongside your responses;

  • Not used for profiling, advertising or any automated decision producing legal effects in the sense of GDPR Art. 22;
  • Withdrawable at any time — your subsequent data will not be processed, but past PDF reports already delivered cannot be recalled.

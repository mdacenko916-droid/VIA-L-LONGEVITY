What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function correctly, remember your preferences, and collect usage data.

Your Choices

When you first visit VIA-L, a cookie banner allows you to accept all cookies or accept essential cookies only. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the site, or through your browser settings.

Most browsers allow you to manage cookies via their settings menu. Note that disabling cookies may affect certain website features.

Third-Party Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies on our behalf to help us understand how visitors interact with the site. Google may transfer this data to servers in other countries. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

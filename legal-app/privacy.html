Privacy Policy

VIA·L AI assistant · Last updated: 25 August 2026

This policy explains how the VIA·L AI assistant app handles your information. The app is a general wellness and educational tool. It is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and is not a substitute for a qualified professional.

1. Who we are

The controller of your data is Maryna Datsenko, an Individual Entrepreneur (FOP) registered in Ukraine ("we", "us"). We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer; all privacy requests are handled directly by the controller.

Privacy contact: privacy@via-l.com · General support: support@via-l.com

2. Scope

This policy applies only to the VIA·L AI assistant mobile application and its subscription. It does not cover any separate website or service.

3. What the app is

The app lets you record self-reported wellness markers (such as sleep quality, energy, activity, nutrition habits and personal goals) and, optionally read wellness metrics from Apple Health or Health Connect with your permission. It uses an artificial-intelligence model to return general, non-medical nutrition and lifestyle suggestions for education and self-care.

4. Information we process

  • Wellness inputs you enter — subjective self-ratings and lifestyle markers you choose to provide.
  • Wearable metrics (optional) — data you explicitly authorise the app to read from Apple Health / Health Connect on your device.
  • Your goals and focus areas — to personalise the suggestions.
  • Minimal technical data — app version and the network request needed to generate an AI response.

The app has no account and no login. We do not ask for your name, email or contact details to use it.

Because these inputs can relate to your physical wellbeing, we treat them as a special category of personal data and process them only with your explicit consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a) and Article 9(2)(a)). You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your data in the app or uninstalling it.

5. Where your data is stored

Your wellness inputs and history are stored locally on your device, in encrypted form, and are not kept permanently on our servers (see the temporary safeguard below). To generate an AI suggestion, the relevant inputs for that request are sent over an encrypted connection to our processing service and to the AI provider, used only to produce your response, and are not retained beyond the short technical lifetime of the request.

A generated overview is additionally kept on our processing service for no longer than 72 hours and is then deleted automatically. This is a safeguard for the case where the response never reached your device (the connection dropped, the app was closed): you can retrieve your own overview without requesting a new one. The record is pseudonymous — it is tied to a random device identifier, with no name, email or access code.

Optional: helping to improve the app. Separately from the above, you can turn on «Help improve VIA·L» — it is off unless you switch it on. While it is on, the numbers of each finished day (tracker metrics, your ratings, home blood-pressure readings, weight and waist, the schema and weight versions) are sent to our processing service and stored there under the same random device identifier, so the app can be calibrated on real data. Anything written in your own words is refused by the server and never leaves the device. Turning the switch off stops it immediately; «Delete my contribution» erases everything already sent from this installation. Legal basis: your explicit consent — GDPR Article 6(1)(a) with Article 9(2)(a). The full text is on the «Help improve VIA·L» page in the app.

6. Service providers

  • Anthropic PBC (USA) — provides the AI model (Claude) that generates suggestions from the inputs sent for a request.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) — hosts the request-relay service; any temporary technical storage uses automatic expiry.
  • Apple / Google — distribute the app and provide the on-device health-data permission (Apple Health / Health Connect). They receive only what their platforms require for app distribution.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for advertising.

7. International transfers

The providers above may process request data in the United States. Such transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and each provider's supplementary safeguards.

8. Retention

On-device data remains until you clear it in the app or uninstall the app — you are in control. Data sent for an AI request is transient and expires automatically (typically within days). We keep no wellness history on our own servers.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because your data lives on your device, you can exercise most of these directly in the app at any time.

EU/UK (GDPR): you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. California (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know, delete and opt out of "sale"/"sharing" — we do neither.

Washington State (My Health My Data Act): you have the right to delete your consumer health data — not merely your account. The app does this directly: “Delete my health data” in the “Backup and data deletion” section erases everything from this device immediately. “Save backup” next to it exports an encrypted copy first. We do not sell or share health data and do not use it to train models.

To make a request, contact privacy@via-l.com. We respond within 30 days (15 days for California requests).

10. Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has used the app, contact privacy@via-l.com and we will assist.

11. Security

Wellness data is encrypted at rest on your device and sent over encrypted (TLS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we apply measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, highlighted in the app.

13. Contact

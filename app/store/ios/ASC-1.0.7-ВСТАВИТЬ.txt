🔴 ДО ПОДАЧИ ОБЯЗАТЕЛЬНО — App Privacy в ASC (раздел «App Privacy» → Edit). Без этого
отказ почти гарантирован: приложение теперь по желанию человека передаёт данные о здоровье
специалисту. Отметить ДОПОЛНИТЕЛЬНО к тому, что уже стоит:
- Health & Fitness → Health (показатели дня, в т.ч. из Apple Health) и Fitness.
- User Content → Other User Content (сообщения специалисту) и Photos or Videos (фото в чате).
- Contact Info → Email Address и Name (уже собирались при подключении — проверить, что стоят).
Для каждого: «Linked to the user» = YES (связано с почтой), «Used for tracking» = NO,
цель — App Functionality. Собираются только если человек сам подключился к специалисту.

APP REVIEW NOTES — English (вставить в App Review Information → Notes, ДОПИСАТЬ к прежним)
----
My guide (optional). Some users work one-on-one with an independent nutrition specialist. On the My guide screen they can enter the specialist's personal code to connect with them. This code does not unlock any paid feature or content in the app — the app works the same with or without it, and the app subscription is purchased only through In-App Purchase.

After connecting, the user can exchange messages and photos with their specialist and can choose, with a separate switch, whether their daily check-ins are shared with that specialist. Sharing can be paused or consent withdrawn at any time on the same screen. Health data (including data read from Apple Health) is shared only with the specialist the user chose, with explicit consent, and is never used for advertising or sold.

Consultations are a person-to-person service provided by the specialist (Guideline 3.1.3(d)); they are arranged and paid directly between the user and the specialist outside the app. The app shows no prices, payment links or purchase buttons for these services.

To test: open My guide, enter the code FOUNDER, any email, tick consent, tap Connect. Then tap Message to open the conversation.
----

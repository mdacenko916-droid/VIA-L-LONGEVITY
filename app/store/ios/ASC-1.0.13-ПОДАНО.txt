REVIEW NOTES (добавлено):

NEW IN 1.0.13 — "Together" (optional pairing between two users).
On the Today screen, under "Focus of the week", a user can invite one person they choose (a partner, friend or family member) with a 6-character one-time code, or enter a code they received. Once paired, each person sees ONLY the display name the other typed, the other's current weekly step (one of nine fixed lifestyle steps written by us) and seven daily "done / not done" marks. No health data, analyses, entries or free text are exchanged, and there is no messaging between the two users. Either person can leave the pair at any time with one tap ("Leave the pair"), which immediately removes the link for both. Optionally, if both are connected to the same independent specialist and both turn on "Show my specialist we are a pair", the specialist sees the two existing cards linked. Each person keeps their own subscription; nothing is purchased or unlocked for the other. Privacy policy updated (section "Optional: Together").
Oura: the app now also requests the Oura "tag" scope to read tags the user entered in the Oura app; they are used only in that user's own analysis. A "Disconnect Oura" button removes our stored access.

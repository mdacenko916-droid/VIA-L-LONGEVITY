APP REVIEW NOTES
----
VIA-L is a wellness app for adults going through midlife hormonal change — perimenopause in women from 35, andropause in men from 40. The user records how they slept, how much energy they had and how they felt; the app describes, in plain language, what appears connected to what and suggests one lifestyle change to try. A wearable is optional: the app works entirely on manually entered data, and Apple Health is only a convenience for filling those fields in.

It does not diagnose, treat or prevent disease and is not a medical device. The disclaimer is shown before first use and repeated on every result.

Access: the first three days are a free in-app preview with a shorter overview. From the fourth day the paywall appears (or open My profile → Subscription). Full access is sold only through In-App Purchase: VIA-L Monthly and VIA-L Yearly (auto-renewable, one group) and VIA-L Lifetime (non-consumable). Restore Purchases is on the paywall. There is no login.

NEW IN THIS VERSION — Specialist review (consumable In-App Purchase, product ID via_l_specialist_review).
On the My guide screen, a user who is not connected to a specialist sees the block "Specialist review" with the price loaded from the App Store. It opens a screen that states exactly what is included, asks for an email and explicit consent to share data, and the Pay button starts the In-App Purchase. After purchase a human nutrition specialist reads the data the user agreed to share and replies in writing in the in-app chat within 48 hours with a written review and a personal lifestyle plan. The user then has 7 days for follow-up questions, after which the chat is closed to new messages and data sharing stops. If the specialist does not reply in time, the app tells the user how to request a refund from Apple.
This is a wellness and lifestyle consultation, not a medical service: the specialist does not diagnose or prescribe medication, and the purchase screen says so. Because the service is delivered in writing inside the app, it is sold only through In-App Purchase; there is no external payment or link.

HOW TO SEE IT: this service is not offered in the United States (several US states restrict nutrition counselling without a state licence), so the block is hidden when the device time zone is a US one. Please set the device time zone to a non-US zone, e.g. Madrid (Settings → General → Date & Time → turn off Set Automatically), force-quit and reopen the app, then open My guide: the block is under the Connect button. A screenshot of the purchase screen is attached to the In-App Purchase.

My guide (optional, free). A user who already works with an independent nutrition specialist can enter that specialist's personal code to connect: exchange messages and photos, and choose with a separate switch whether daily check-ins are shared. The code unlocks no paid feature. Ongoing consultations with an independent specialist are a person-to-person service arranged and paid directly between them outside the app (Guideline 3.1.3(d)); the app shows no prices or payment links for those. To test: open My guide, enter the code FOUNDER, any email, tick consent, tap Connect, then tap Message.

Health data: HealthKit is requested only after the user is told what it is used for, read-only. Data is stored on the device. It is shared only with a specialist the user chose or purchased a review from, with explicit consent, and is never used for advertising or sold.
Support: support@via-l.com

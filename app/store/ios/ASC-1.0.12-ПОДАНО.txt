REVIEW NOTES:
VIA-L is a wellness app for adults going through midlife hormonal change — perimenopause in women from 35, andropause in men from 40. The user records how they slept, how much energy they had and how they felt; the app describes, in plain language, what appears connected to what and suggests one lifestyle change to try. A wearable is optional: the app works fully on manually entered data; Apple Health only fills those fields in.

It does not diagnose, treat or prevent disease and is not a medical device. The disclaimer is shown before first use and repeated on every result.

Access: the first seven days are a free in-app preview. After that the paywall appears (or Profile → Subscription). Full access is sold only through In-App Purchase: VIA-L Monthly and VIA-L Yearly (auto-renewable, one group) and VIA-L Lifetime (non-consumable). Restore Purchases is on the paywall. There is no login.

NEW IN 1.0.12 (all inside Workout of the day — general fitness guidance, not treatment)

1) My sports (Profile → My data → My sports): the user picks sports they already do (padel, cycling, football…) and their usual days. On those days the workout card says the sport is the day's activity and offers a warm-up and a stretch; yesterday's sport is counted as load.

2) Before starting a workout: two optional questions — what the user has already done today and when they last ate. If they already played a sport, the workout switches to muscles that rested (the original plan stays one tap away). "Get from my watch" reads today's workouts from Apple Health (read-only) to fill this in.

3) When the wearable's recovery reading and the user's own rating disagree, the card names both and suggests an easier pace; it never claims one of them is right.

4) Player: equipment and how to choose dumbbell weight at the start, a 3-2-1 countdown at the end of rest, short encouraging phrases; "How did it go" can store the dumbbell weight used, kept on the device.

Unchanged: lab results from a photo (Profile → My data → Lab tests → Scan from photo). The photo is sent only to read the numbers and is not stored; values are confirmed by the user and kept on the device; each value is compared only with the range printed on the user's own report.

Specialist review (consumable IAP via_l_specialist_review, approved with 1.0.9): hidden when the device time zone is a US one — to see it, set e.g. Madrid, reopen the app, open the Specialist tab.

Specialist tab, connecting to a personal specialist (optional, free): code FOUNDER, any email, tick consent, Connect. The code unlocks no paid feature; ongoing consultations are arranged and paid outside the app (Guideline 3.1.3(d)); the app shows no prices or payment links for them.

Health data: HealthKit is read-only and requested only after explaining its use. Data stays on the device; it is shared only with a specialist the user chose or bought a review from, with explicit consent, and is never used for advertising or sold.

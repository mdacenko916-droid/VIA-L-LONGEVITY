Subject: Recommending Oura to our members — referral partnership (no API needed)

Hi Becky,

We spoke earlier this year about a technical partnership (ticket #6994484), and I
completely understand that the partner programme was closed at the time. I'm coming
back with something much simpler, and I'm writing to you directly because our notes to
the general sales address haven't found their way to a person yet.

A short reminder of who we are. VIA-L is a wellness platform that helps people in their
late thirties and beyond make sense of what their tracker already measures — sleep,
HRV, resting heart rate, activity, temperature trends — and turn it into plain-language
weekly guidance. We work in twelve languages, and alongside the self-guided product our
members can work with a specialist who reads the same data with them.

Which brings us to the ask. Every week we are asked the same question: *"which ring
should I buy?"* Our answer is already Oura — it is the device our members have the best
experience with, and the one we recommend first. What we don't have is a proper way to
make that recommendation worth something to Oura or to the person buying.

So we would like to become a referral partner:

- a **partner link** to your store that we place inside the product and in the
  onboarding materials our members read before they buy anything;
- a **discount code** we can hand to our members, so the recommendation arrives with a
  real benefit attached rather than as bare advice.

That's the whole request — no API, no data access, nothing on your engineering team's
plate. Just a link and a code, and whatever attribution and commercial terms your
referral programme normally runs on.

For scale: we are early and honest about it — the order of magnitude today is dozens of
buyers per year, growing as the platform opens to more markets. If our volume is below
the threshold for the formal programme, we would still gladly start with a plain
referral link and revisit later.

If this belongs with a colleague or with a different programme entirely, I would be
grateful if you could point me to the right door — that alone would help us a great
deal.

Thank you for your time, and for the work you do — the ring genuinely earns the
recommendation.

Warm regards,

## Ответ Oura — 🔴 ОТКАЗ (2026-08-26)

Тикет **#7805684**. Ответила не Becky, а **Dante, Oura Member Success Support** — то есть

Суть: у Oura **нет действующей реферальной программы для объёмов меньше 20 колец** — ни
партнёрской ссылки, ни условий атрибуции, ни бизнес-кода скидки. Покупать наши люди
могут только через обычный онлайн-магазин Oura. Предложил помочь с вопросами по
продукту — то есть дверь в партнёрство отсюда не открывается.

**Что из этого следует:**
- порог назван прямым числом — **20 устройств**. Это тот же порог, что и в отказе Becky
  по API (партнёрство >10, тикет #6994484): Oura пускает в партнёрскую дверь только на
  объёме, и разговор имеет смысл возобновлять, когда сможем показать 20+ покупателей;
- ветка «Oura for Organizations» (заявка 2026-07-29, объём «5-19») этим ответом фактически
  закрыта — мы попали ровно под порог;
- до тех пор в онбординге **обычная ссылка на магазин Oura, без кода и без выгоды** —
  ничего не обещаем пользователю про скидку;
- интеграция Oura по OAuth работает и от этого отказа не зависит — см.
  `interpreter/wearables/oura.md`.

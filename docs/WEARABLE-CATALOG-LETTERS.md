Тема: Re: VIA-L — request to be considered for the Oura Integrations directory (Women's Health)

**EN**

Hi Becky,

Following up on our conversation from June. Since then, our Oura integration has gone live: the Oura
developer team approved VIA-L for production access in August (ticket #7815321), and the app is now
available on the App Store, with Android on the way.

VIA-L helps women from 35 going through perimenopause (and men from 40 in andropause) understand what
their Oura data means in this specific period: sleep, HRV, resting heart rate and temperature are read
against the person's own baseline and cycle phase, and turned into one small weekly step built around
the symptom that bothers them most. It is a wellness app, not a medical device; Oura data is used only
to produce the user's own analysis and is never used to train or improve any AI model.

We would like to ask how VIA-L could be considered for the Integrations directory in the Oura app,
in the Women's Health category. Happy to send screenshots, a test account or anything else your team
needs for review.

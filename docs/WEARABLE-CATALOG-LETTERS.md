Hi Helder,

Thank you again for approving VIA-L in August. I am writing with a request that matters a great deal to
us, and I would be very grateful if you could forward this message to the team that manages the
Integrations directory in the Oura app.

We are a family from Ukraine. The war made us refugees, and we lost everything we had: our home, our
work, our former life. Starting from zero, we built VIA-L — a new product for people 40+ going through
one of the hardest transitions in life: perimenopause, menopause and andropause. Millions of people in
this period feel unwell and get little support. Sleep, energy, mood and weight change, and nobody
connects the dots.

Our Oura integration is already live: on 26 August you approved VIA-L for production access to the Oura
API and lifted the 10-user limit. The app is on the App Store in 12
languages, with Android in final testing.

What VIA-L does with an Oura ring:
• Reads sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, temperature and activity against the person's own baseline,
  not generic norms.
• Uses the ring's night temperature to confirm the cycle phase — and, when cycles become irregular in
  perimenopause, stops relying on the phase instead of giving wrong advice.
• Combines the ring with what the person feels: hot flashes, sleep quality, mood, energy and their main
  complaint, which drives the whole review.
• Adds lab results (photographed by the user), blood pressure, a supplement and medication schedule with
  reminders, and a daily food plan with concrete meals adjusted to their habits.
• Turns all of this into a personal daily plan, a weekly review that explains what changed and why, and
  one clear focus for the week with short day-by-day guides. When needed, users can order a written
  review from a nutrition specialist inside the app.
• Our patterns are backed by an evidence base of 970 studies, shown to the user under every review.

Why it is different from what your Women's Health category offers today: the apps there focus on cycle
tracking and fertility. VIA-L picks up where they stop — the years when the cycle becomes unpredictable
and the body changes. It is also built for men from 40 going through andropause, who have almost nothing
of this kind. And it gives Oura members a reason to keep wearing the ring through this period, because
their data finally gets explained in context.

VIA-L is a wellness tool, not a medical device and not a diagnosis. Oura data is used only to produce the
user's own analysis and is never used to train or improve any AI model, in line with the API Agreement.

For a self-funded startup like ours, being listed in the Oura app would matter more than any advertising
we could afford: it would let us reach exactly the people we built VIA-L for. Could you tell us how VIA-L
could be considered for the Integrations directory, in the Women's Health category? We will gladly send
screenshots, a test account or a short demo — whatever your team needs.

Thank you for your time,
VIA-L team
integration@via-l.com · https://via-l.com

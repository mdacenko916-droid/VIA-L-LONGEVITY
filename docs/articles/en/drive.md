# No drive: where the energy of wanting goes and how to get it back

*4 min read*

You used to want to take on new things, argue, make plans. Now it's "I don't feel like anything", even things that used to bring joy. It isn't necessarily a midlife crisis, nor a verdict that "the hormones have run out". Most often three clear things are dampening your drive.

## What dampens drive the most

**Short sleep.** Short and irregular sleep raises stress hormones and lowers testosterone.

**Chronic stress and overload.** The body switches to saving mode: under heavy strain, testosterone can fall by 60–70% in a week. And it's reversible — it comes back within days to weeks once the strain eases. It's adaptation, not a breakdown.

**Extra weight around the waist.** Belly fat lowers testosterone by itself and steals your energy.

So the place to start isn't "testosterone boosters", but these three causes.

## When it may be your mood

If you've lost interest in almost everything, become irritable and feel drawn to alcohol — this may be depression, which in men often looks exactly like this. More in the article "Irritability, apathy, 'nothing brings joy'".

## What brings your drive back

**Sleep.** Get up at the same time all seven days, 7 or more hours.

**Movement.** Strength and aerobic exercise improve mood, desire and energy. For a man over 40, the risk of moving too little is far higher than the risk of "overdoing it".

**Your waist.** Losing 5–10% of your weight raises testosterone roughly in proportion and brings your energy back.

**An evening without alcohol.** It lowers testosterone and cuts into the second half of the night.

**Coffee in the morning.** Daytime coffee keeps stress hormones high.

**Light and people.** Daylight in the morning and real conversation are simple but real levers.

## What to avoid

- **"Testosterone boosters".** Almost none have evidence of benefit, and some contain undeclared drugs.
- **Testosterone "for drive" without tests.** It's prescribed only for a confirmed deficiency — after two morning tests and a doctor's decision. Hormone from outside suppresses sperm production.

## When to see a doctor

- You've lost interest in almost everything for more than two weeks.
- Along with this — new erection problems, enlarged breast tissue or changes in the testicles.
- Thoughts of harming yourself — get help from a professional or a crisis line right away.

## What to try this week

- Get up at the same time all seven days.
- Two 30–40 minute workouts on different days.
- An evening without alcohol.

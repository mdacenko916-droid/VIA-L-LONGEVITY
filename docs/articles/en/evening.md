# Coffee before 2 pm, no evening alcohol, early dinner: why it works

*3 min read*

This is one of the simplest steps in the "Focus of the week" — and one of the most effective for sleep. It is not about adding something, but about removing three things that quietly spoil the night.

## Why it works

**Coffee keeps the body "switched on".** Caffeine raises stress hormones and keeps them from falling by evening. It acts for a long time, so a cup at four in the afternoon makes it hard to fall asleep at midnight. The cut-off is around 2 pm, that is, 8–9 hours before bed.

**Alcohol is a "false friend".** A glass in the evening helps you fall asleep but breaks up the second half of the night: more awakenings, poorer recovery. It makes hot flashes worse even in small amounts, and in perimenopause it unsettles anxiety — calming you for an hour, then making things worse.

**A late dinner keeps the body from cooling down and settling.** Your last heavy meal — three hours before bed.

## How to do it

- **Coffee and strong tea only before 2 pm.** After that — herbal tea, water, decaf.
- **A one-week break from evening alcohol.** Not forever, just for a week — to see the difference for yourself.
- **Dinner three hours before bed.** If you go to bed at 11 pm, have dinner before 8 pm.

## What can get in the way

- The habit of "coffee after lunch so I don't fall asleep". Instead — a 10-minute walk outside.
- A glass of wine "to relax". Try a replacement ritual: a warm shower, a walk, slow breathing.
- Getting home late from work. Then — a light dinner, with your main meal earlier in the day.

## What to expect

Look not at one night but at a week: how quickly you fall asleep, how many times you wake, how you feel in the morning. Log your sleep and hot flashes in VIA-L — that way you will see the difference in numbers, not just in how you feel.

## Who this step helps

Those with poor sleep, night-time hot flashes and morning tiredness. It often goes together with the step "Same wake-up time and morning light".

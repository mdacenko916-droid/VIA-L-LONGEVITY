# Coffee before lunch, no evening alcohol, early dinner: why it works

*3 min read*

This is one of the simplest steps in the "Focus of the week" and one of the most effective for sleep. It isn't about adding something — it's about removing three things that quietly ruin the night.

## Why it works

**Coffee keeps stress hormones high.** The body partly gets used to the morning cup, but afternoon coffee keeps pushing stress hormones and cuts deep sleep. The cut-off is early in the day: the last cup 8 or more hours before bed.

**Alcohol hits three times.** It relaxes the throat muscles and makes snoring and apnea worse, lowers testosterone and cuts the second half of the night. A "nightcap" helps you fall asleep, but it makes sleep worse rather than fixing it. Even two glasses.

**A late dinner works against the body.** In the evening the body handles sugar less well, so the main meal is better earlier. The last heavy meal — three hours before bed.

## How to do it

- **Coffee and strong tea — only in the first half of the day.** After that — water, herbal tea, decaf.
- **Evening alcohol — a one-week pause.** Not forever, just for a week — to see the difference for yourself.
- **Dinner — three hours before bed.** The main meal during the day, something lighter in the evening.

## What can get in the way

- The habit of "coffee after lunch so I don't nod off". Instead — a 10-minute walk outside.
- A beer or a glass "to unwind". Try a replacement ritual: a warm shower, a walk, slow breathing.
- Getting home late from work. Then — a light dinner, and the main meal earlier, during the day.

## What to expect

Look at a week, not one night: how quickly you fall asleep, how many times you wake up, how you feel in the morning. If your partner has noticed snoring — ask whether it has got quieter. Log your sleep in VIA-L.

## Who this step helps

People with poor sleep, snoring, morning grogginess and low energy. It works well together with the step "Same wake-up time and morning light".

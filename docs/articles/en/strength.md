# Strength training twice a week: one step, benefits for everything

*4 min read*

If you could choose only one piece of advice for the transition, it would be strength training. It works on hot flashes, mood, bones, muscles, metabolism and sleep all at once. And you don't need a gym for it.

## Why it works

- **Hot flashes.** Strength training is the strongest lever without medication: in the best study, hot flashes roughly halved.
- **Mood.** It improves even before strength and weight have changed.
- **Bones.** Load is the main signal for bone to stay strong; strength training together with a little jumping works best.
- **Muscles and energy.** After 40, muscle responds less well to load — so it needs more, not less.
- **Metabolism.** Muscle is the main "consumer" of sugar; strength training also lowers background inflammation, even without weight loss.
- **Brain.** Movement improves attention and clarity.

## How to do it

- **Two sessions of 30–40 minutes on different days.**
- **The basics are squats, pulls and presses.** For example: squats to a chair, pulling a resistance band towards your waist, push-ups against a wall or table, step-ups, overhead dumbbell presses.
- **Without pain.** The load should be noticeable, but not painful.
- **Build up gradually.** When it gets easy — a little more weight or more repetitions.

With a trainer, results and safety are better. VIA-L has a voice-guided "Workout of the day" — you can start with that.

## Who should take more care

- **High blood pressure:** don't hold your breath during effort — breathe out as you lift. After floor exercises, get up slowly.
- **Diabetes medication:** with new exercise, blood sugar can drop more than usual. Keep a quick snack to hand, and stop if you feel weak or shaky.
- **Joint or back pain:** if an exercise causes pain, replace it with an easier one or reduce the weight.

If you are not sure whether the exercise is right for you, discuss it with your doctor.

## What can get in the way

- "I don't have time." Two 30-minute sessions is an hour a week.
- "I've never done this." Start with your own body weight and a chair. Regularity matters, not records.

## What to expect

Mood and how you feel often change before strength and figure do. Log your workouts, hot flashes and mood in VIA-L — in a few weeks you will see what is changing for you.

## Who this step helps

Almost everyone: with hot flashes, tiredness, mood swings, a growing waist, aching joints and muscle weakness.

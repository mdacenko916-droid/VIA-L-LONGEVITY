# Vegetables and protein before carbs: the same lunch, gentler on blood sugar

*3 min read*

This step is not about eating less or giving up foods you love. It is about order: what to eat first. A simple rearrangement on your plate noticeably changes how your body copes with food.

## Why it works

If you start a meal with vegetables and protein, and eat bread, grains, potatoes or pasta afterwards, the sugar spike after the meal is gentler — by about a third. A smaller spike means a smaller slump afterwards: less drowsiness after lunch and fewer sugar cravings.

In the transition the body responds less well to sugar, so simple techniques like this are especially useful. And together with a walk after meals, the effects add up.

## How to do it

- **Vegetables first** — salad, cooked or raw vegetables.
- **Then protein** — meat, fish, eggs, tofu, pulses.
- **Carbohydrates last:** the side dish, the bread.

All on one plate, simply in this order. You don't need to eat less.

## Who should take more care

- **If you have had difficulties with food** — an eating disorder now or in the past — food rules are better discussed with a specialist than introduced on your own.
- **If you take warfarin:** don't change the amount of green vegetables suddenly — it affects how the medicine works. Discuss any changes in your diet with your doctor.

## What can get in the way

- "I have no vegetables." Frozen ones or a simple plate of cucumber, tomato and pepper will do.
- "Eating out or at friends'." Start with a salad or a vegetable starter, bread afterwards.
- "Breakfast is porridge or a sandwich." Add an egg, cottage cheese or vegetables to it first.

## What to expect

Notice how full you feel after meals, your after-lunch drowsiness and sugar cravings. Weight changes more slowly, so go by how you feel. Log it in VIA-L.

## Who this step helps

Those with a growing waist, energy slumps after meals and sugar cravings. It works well together with a walk after meals and protein at every meal.

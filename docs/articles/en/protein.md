# Protein at every meal: why you need more after 40

*3 min read*

In the morning, coffee and a biscuit; during the day, something on the go; in the evening, a proper dinner. A great many people eat like this. And in this pattern there is almost no protein until the evening. After 40 this noticeably affects energy, muscles and sugar cravings.

## Why it works

**Muscle has become "more demanding".** After 40 it responds less well to protein, so to keep it you need more protein than before — and spread evenly through the day, not all at dinner.

**Protein brings fullness and steady blood sugar.** A protein-rich meal keeps you full for longer and smooths out sugar spikes — fewer slumps and fewer sugar cravings.

**A protein breakfast evens out the day.** A skipped or "empty" breakfast keeps stress hormones high, and a slump follows during the day. It is better to have breakfast within an hour or two of waking.

## How to do it

- **A source of protein at every meal.** Aim for 30–40 g of protein per meal.
- **Start with breakfast:** that is usually where there is least protein.
- **Over the day** — at least 1–1.2 g of protein per kilogram of body weight, more if you do strength training.

What 30–40 g of protein looks like in practice — roughly:

- breakfast: an omelette of 2–3 eggs with cottage cheese, or Greek yoghurt with nuts;
- lunch: a palm-sized portion of fish, chicken or meat;
- dinner: lentils or beans with tofu, cheese, seafood.

Protein powder is a handy back-up if you can't get enough from food.

## Who should take more care

- **With kidney disease**, agree the amount of protein with your doctor.
- **If you have had difficulties with food** — an eating disorder now or in the past — food rules are better discussed with a specialist than introduced on your own.

## What can get in the way

- "I can't eat in the morning." Start small: a yoghurt, an egg, a glass of kefir.
- "I don't have time to cook." Boiled eggs, cottage cheese, tinned fish, ready-cooked lentils — no cooking needed.

## What to expect

Notice how fullness and sugar cravings between meals change. Log your energy and how you feel in VIA-L.

## Who this step helps

Those with tiredness, a growing waist, muscle weakness and aching joints. It works well together with strength training.

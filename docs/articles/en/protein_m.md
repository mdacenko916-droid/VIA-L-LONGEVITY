# Protein at every meal: why you need more after 40

*3 min read*

Coffee in the morning, something on the run during the day, a big dinner in the evening. That's how a great many men eat. And there's almost no protein in this pattern until the evening. After 40 this shows noticeably in muscles, energy and the belly.

## Why it works

**Muscle has become more "demanding".** With age it responds less well to protein, so to keep it you need more protein than before — and spread evenly through the day, not all at dinner.

**Protein holds on to muscle during weight loss.** When the waistline shrinks, it matters that fat goes, not muscle. Protein and strength training are exactly what take care of that.

**Protein keeps you full.** Protein-rich food keeps you satisfied for a long time. Ultra-processed snacks, which are low in protein, push you to overeat by themselves.

## How to do it

- **A source of protein at every meal.** A guide — 30–40 g of protein per meal.
- **Start with breakfast:** that's usually where protein is lowest.
- **Per day** — 1.2–1.6 g of protein per kilogram of body weight.

What 30–40 g of protein looks like in practice — roughly:

- breakfast: a 3-egg omelette with cottage cheese, or Greek yogurt with nuts;
- lunch: a palm-sized portion of fish, chicken or meat;
- dinner: lentils or beans with cheese, tofu, seafood.

Protein powder is a handy back-up if you can't get enough from food.

## Who should be more careful

- **With kidney disease**, always agree the amount of protein with your doctor.
- **If you have had difficulties with food** — an eating disorder now or in the past — rules about food are better discussed with a specialist than introduced on your own.

## What can get in the way

- "I can't eat in the morning". Start small: yogurt, a couple of eggs, a glass of kefir.
- "No time to cook". Boiled eggs, cottage cheese, tinned fish, ready-cooked lentils — no cooking needed.

## What to expect

Watch how fullness between meals and strength in workouts change. Log how you feel in VIA-L. Works well paired with strength training.

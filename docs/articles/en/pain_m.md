# Aches and stiffness in men after 40: where they come from and what helps

*4 min read*

In the morning your body takes a while to get going. Your knees, back and shoulders ache — though nothing happened. People usually blame age and the sports of their youth. But this kind of aching often has a clear and fixable cause.

## Why it aches

In men over 40, aches and stiffness often go hand in hand with low-grade inflammation. Its main driver is fat around the waist: it constantly releases substances that keep inflammation going throughout the body. That's why joints, sleep, fatigue and mood so often drag each other down.

## Rest or move?

Move. Muscle is an active anti-inflammatory organ. Regular training lowers low-grade inflammation, and the small "spike" after a workout is a normal part of adapting. Only overload without recovery does harm.

## What helps

**Regular movement and strength training.** 150 minutes a week plus strength 2–3 times — without pain.

**Your waist.** Reducing fat around the waist is the main lever against low-grade inflammation.

**Don't smoke.** Even one cigarette a day keeps inflammation going; after quitting it returns to normal in about a year.

**Sleep.** Several nights of short sleep in a row raise inflammation.

**A Mediterranean style.** Olive oil, nuts, legumes, fish, vegetables; less processed meat and fewer sugary drinks.

**Teeth and gums.** Gum inflammation is an underrated source of inflammation throughout the body. See a dentist regularly.

## About bones — the part that doesn't hurt

Osteoporosis is thought of as a women's issue, but it's common in men too and found less often. And after a hip fracture, outcomes are worse in men. Bone is lost without symptoms. Risk factors: low testosterone, long-term steroids, low body weight, smoking, heavy drinking, a fracture from a minor injury, losing height. With these, it's worth discussing a bone density scan with a doctor. Testosterone therapy doesn't protect bone from fractures — what protects it is strength and impact exercise, protein, vitamin D and calcium.

## What about supplements?

"Anti-inflammatory" supplements lower markers in blood tests, but none has proven a health benefit. Curcumin can damage the liver and interacts with medicines. Boswellia helps only with knee pain from osteoarthritis.

## When to see a doctor without delay

- A joint is swollen, and morning stiffness lasts longer than an hour.
- After 50 — pain and stiffness in both shoulders or hips and feeling unwell. If this comes with a new headache, vision problems or jaw pain when chewing — urgently.
- A suddenly swollen, red, hot and very painful joint.
- Unexplained weight loss, fever and drenching night sweats.
- Any fracture from a minor injury.

## What to try this week

- Two 30–40 minute strength workouts on different days, without pain.
- A 10–15 minute walk after your main meals.
- Book a dentist appointment if it's been a while.

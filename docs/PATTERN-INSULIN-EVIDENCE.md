# P-F7 Инсулинорезистентность / «как тело отвечает на сахар» — углубление: вопросы для OpenEvidence

Продолжение метаболической цепочки из P-F1 (эстроген↓ → висцеральный жир → инсулин → тяга/туман/вес).
Цель: ИИ должен РАСПОЗНАВАТЬ инсулиновый паттерн по нашим сигналам (без глюкометра/CGM) и давать
доказательные рычаги с величинами — дескриптивно, без диагноза «инсулинорезистентность/преддиабет».

## Что уже есть
- **Клинический KB** P-F7 (~L49): богатый (меноожирение, талия/рост, белок+силовые, NEAT, диет-тренды).
- **Велнес-KB** P-F7 (~L738): одна строка (клетчатка/белок/ходьба после еды/среднеземноморье) — нет
  менопаузальной специфики, распознавания по нашим сигналам, величин рычагов.
- Данные до ИИ доходят: вес/талия/WHtR, энергия, тяга к сладкому, аппетит, туман, сон, стресс, движение.

## Вопросы для OpenEvidence (English)

### A. Physiology & menopause-specificity
1. How does the menopause transition (estrogen loss) affect insulin sensitivity **independent of aging and weight** — magnitude, mechanisms (ERα in muscle/liver/adipose, visceral fat, falling SHBG/rising free testosterone, FSH), and how it links to the visceral-fat cascade?
2. What is the **bidirectional relationship between sleep** (short/poor sleep, fragmentation, circadian disruption) and insulin sensitivity/glucose control — quantified?
3. How do resting heart rate, HRV, and chronic stress/cortisol relate to insulin resistance in midlife women?

### B. Recognition from consumer signals (no glucose meter / CGM)
4. What everyday, **non-lab signals** correlate with insulin resistance in midlife women — post-meal energy crashes/sleepiness, sugar/carb cravings, brain fog, afternoon dips, waist circumference/WHtR, energy patterns — and how reliable is each as a marker?
5. What **waist-circumference / waist-to-height** thresholds best flag elevated cardiometabolic/insulin risk in women (including ethnicity-specific cutoffs), and why WHtR outperforms BMI?
6. How does insulin resistance manifest in **energy, mood, and cognition** (the "afternoon crash", cravings, brain fog) — the physiological basis linking glucose swings to these subjective symptoms?

### C. Solutions — nutrition & lifestyle levers (magnitudes)
7. **Protein at each meal** for midlife women: how much (g/meal, g/kg/day), and its effect on glucose response, satiety, muscle preservation, and cravings?
8. **Dietary fiber** and glucose: dose-response effect on post-meal glucose and insulin sensitivity; soluble/viscous vs insoluble; realistic daily targets.
9. **Meal sequencing** (vegetables/protein/fat before carbohydrates) and **post-meal walking**: quantified effect on post-meal glucose excursions.
10. **Exercise** for insulin sensitivity in midlife women: resistance vs aerobic vs post-meal walking vs NEAT — effect sizes and practical dosing; and the caution around fasted training in women.
11. Which **foods/nutrients** have real evidence for glucose/insulin (Mediterranean pattern, vinegar before meals, cinnamon, magnesium, omega-3) and which **supplements** (berberine, myo-inositol, chromium) — effect sizes and wellness safety cautions?
12. **Meal timing / time-restricted eating / breakfast** for midlife women's glucose — early vs late eating window, and the cautions specific to women (stress, muscle, cycle)?

### D. Safety / communication (non-diagnostic wellness)
13. For a consumer wellness (non-diagnostic) app: how to describe "how your body responds to sugar" patterns **without diagnosing insulin resistance / prediabetes / diabetes**, and which red-flag symptoms (of high or low blood sugar) should prompt seeing a clinician?

  провалы/тяга/туман/талия/сон) + рычаги с величинами (белок, клетчатка, порядок еды, ходьба, силовые,
  сон) + честно про добавки (берберин/инозитол — что доказано, что как опция), дескриптивно.
- Связать с P-F1 (единая цепочка эстроген→висцеральный жир→инсулин→тяга/туман/вес).
- Числа — внутренние ориентиры; клиенту качественно, без диагноза.

## EN — текст письма

Subject: Recommending Ultrahuman Ring to our members — referral partnership (no API needed)

Hi Vighnesh,

We were in touch back in June about a technical partnership, and I appreciated how
quickly and warmly your team replied. I'm coming back now with something far simpler
than what we discussed then — and I'm writing to you directly, because notes to the
general partnerships address haven't reached a person since.

A short reminder of who we are. VIA-L is a wellness platform that helps people in their
late thirties and beyond make sense of what their tracker already measures — sleep, HRV,
resting heart rate, activity, temperature trends — and turns it into plain-language
weekly guidance. We work in twelve languages, and alongside the self-guided product our
members can work through the same data with a specialist.

Which brings us to the ask. Every week someone asks us the same question: *"which ring
should I buy?"* Ultrahuman is on the short list we name — and for members who care about
metabolic health it is often the first thing we point to. What we don't have is a way to
make that recommendation worth something to Ultrahuman or to the person buying it.

So we would like to become a referral partner:

- a **partner link** to your store that we place inside the product and in the
  onboarding materials our members read before they buy anything;
- a **discount code** we can hand to our members, so the recommendation arrives with a
  real benefit attached rather than as bare advice.

That is the whole request — no API, no data access, nothing on your engineering team's
plate. Just a link and a code, on whatever attribution and commercial terms your
referral programme normally runs on.

On scale, honestly: we are early — the order of magnitude today is dozens of buyers per
year, growing as the platform opens to more markets. If that is below the threshold for
your formal programme, we would gladly start with a plain referral link and revisit
later.

And if this sits with a colleague or with a different programme entirely, a pointer to
the right door would already help us a great deal.

Thank you for your time — and for building a ring that makes the recommendation an easy
one.

Warm regards,
Ihor Datsenko

# P-F13 Кардиоваскулярный риск в менопаузе — углубление: вопросы для OpenEvidence (14)

Десятый узел, «сосудистый» анкер. Собирает всё метаболическое: инсулин (P-F7), воспаление (P-F8),
давление (уже в опроснике), висцеральный жир, кортизол (P-F4). Ставки высшие: болезни сердца — причина
смерти №1 у женщин, а менопауза = реальное окно ускорения. Особенности: (1) сильные ЖЕНСКИЕ факторы риска
(ранняя менопауза, преэклампсия/ГСД в анамнезе, аутоиммунные, тяжёлые приливы); (2) ⚠️ женщины переносят
инфаркт/стенокардию/инсульт АТИПИЧНО и недодиагностируются — красный флаг здесь спасает жизнь; (3) честная
рамка «менопауза — окно профилактики, а не приговор», без запугивания.

Цель: ИИ должен (1) РАСПОЗНАВАТЬ кластер сердечно-сосудистого риска по нашим сигналам, (2) знать ЖЕНСКИЕ
усилители риска, (3) давать доказательные рычаги (средиземноморье, движение, БП, не курить), (4) честно про
маркёры (ApoB/Lp(a)/АД — измеряет врач) и добавки, (5) КРАСНЫЕ ФЛАГИ острых событий (атипичный инфаркт,
инсульт FAST) → неотложка, и когда к врачу на оценку риска.

## Что уже есть
- **Клинический KB** P-F13 (~L76): богатый (аллостатическая нагрузка/weathering; VMS↑ → ССС-риск ACC 2024;
  акушерский анамнез = «проваленный стресс-тест»; средиземноморье, омега-3 2–4 г, ApoB>ЛПНП, цели ЛПНП по
  риску, АД >120/80 не игнорировать у женщин 40–50; берберин/CoQ10/магний/K2).
- **Велнес-KB** P-F13 (~L744): одна строка (средиземноморье, омега-3, аэробные ~150 мин; показатели — с
  врачом) — нет рамки окна риска, женских факторов, распознавания, честных маркёров, КРАСНЫХ ФЛАГОВ инфаркта/
  инсульта, места HRT.
- Данные до ИИ доходят: сердцебиение (симптом), АД (bp_sys/bp_dia), WHtR/талия, вес, сон, стресс, приливы,
  движение. Лаб (липиды/ApoB) НЕТ → сигнал/анамнез-путь + маркёры «к врачу».

## Вопросы для OpenEvidence (English)

### A. Physiology & menopause-specificity
1. How does estrogen loss across the menopause transition **accelerate cardiovascular risk** — endothelial/vascular changes, the atherogenic lipid shift (rising LDL-C, ApoB, Lp(a), triglycerides), visceral fat, blood pressure, insulin resistance — such that the female "protection gap" versus men narrows after menopause? Is menopause an independent accelerator beyond chronological aging, and what is the timing?
2. Are **vasomotor symptoms (hot flashes/night sweats)** a marker of cardiovascular risk / vascular dysfunction — quantified — and does symptom burden (frequency/severity/persistence) track subclinical atherosclerosis and events?
3. What **female-specific and reproductive-history risk enhancers** should raise cardiovascular concern in midlife women — hypertensive disorders of pregnancy (preeclampsia/gestational HTN), gestational diabetes, preterm/SGA delivery, early menopause/POI (<45), PCOS, and autoimmune disease (RA/SLE) — the "pregnancy as a failed stress test" concept and magnitude of risk?

### B. Recognition & red flags
4. What **everyday/consumer signals** flag elevated cardiovascular risk in midlife women (blood pressure, resting heart rate, palpitations, waist-to-height/central adiposity, weight, poor sleep, VMS burden, low physical activity, depression) — and how should a wellness tool combine them, given that risk is a clinical assessment?
5. ⚠️ **RED FLAGS — acute cardiac events in women:** how do women experience **heart attack and angina differently** (chest pain PLUS or instead: jaw/neck/back/arm pain, nausea/vomiting, shortness of breath, unusual/extreme fatigue, cold sweat, lightheadedness), why are women under-recognized and under-treated, and what warrants **calling emergency services immediately**? Include stroke red flags (FAST).
6. Which **markers actually matter** for midlife women, framed honestly (these require clinical measurement): **ApoB vs LDL-C, Lp(a) (once-in-a-lifetime), non-HDL, triglycerides, hs-CRP**, and blood-pressure thresholds/targets in midlife women (why 120–130 systolic should not be dismissed)?

### C. Solutions — levers with magnitudes
7. **Mediterranean / DASH dietary pattern** for cardiovascular risk in women — effect sizes (PREDIMED and others) on events, lipids, BP; role of olive oil, oily fish, nuts, fiber; and limiting sodium, saturated/trans fat, ultra-processed food, and alcohol.
8. **Exercise** (aerobic + resistance) for cardiovascular risk, lipids, blood pressure, and fitness (VO₂max as vital sign) in midlife women — effect sizes and dosing.
9. **Smoking, alcohol, visceral fat/weight, sleep, and psychosocial stress** as cardiovascular levers in midlife women — quantified effects; and the role of stress/depression given women's greater stress-related CV vulnerability.
10. **Blood-pressure management by lifestyle** (DASH, sodium reduction, potassium, weight loss, exercise, alcohol) — quantified magnitudes in midlife women (cross-link the blood-pressure evidence base).
11. **Supplements & the honest boundary:** omega-3 (EPA/DHA), CoQ10, berberine, red yeast rice, plant sterols, fiber/psyllium, magnesium, vitamin K2 — what the evidence actually shows for cardiovascular/lipid outcomes, safety cautions, and where **statins/prescription therapy** are the evidence-based step (a clinician decision, not our recommendation).

### D. Safety / communication (non-diagnostic wellness)
12. For a consumer wellness (non-diagnostic) app: how to frame **menopause as a cardiovascular prevention window** and encourage "know your numbers" (BP, lipids/ApoB, glucose) and lifestyle — **without diagnosing heart disease and without catastrophizing** or implying inevitability?
13. **Emergency & referral mapping:** acute cardiac symptoms → call emergency now; stroke FAST → emergency; risk-factor clustering / family history of premature CVD / pregnancy complications / early menopause → clinician for formal risk assessment (which tests). How should a wellness tool present these tiers?
14. Where does **menopausal hormone therapy** honestly sit for cardiovascular risk — the "timing hypothesis" (early initiation near menopause for VMS appears CV-neutral to favorable; late initiation harmful), that MHT is **not indicated for CVD prevention**, and how a wellness tool should stay non-prescriptive?
